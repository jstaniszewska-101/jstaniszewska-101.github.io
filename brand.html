Seeing clearly — from within

Clear space: Maintain minimum padding around the logo equal to the width of one spiral circle (approximately the radius of the outer ring). Never place the logo closer than this distance to any edge, text, or competing visual element.

Meaning: Two spiral paths — one rose (outer world), one indigo (inner world) — that converge into a single line. They do not merge into sameness; they arrive together.

Usage

Do

  • Use Cormorant Garamond for all headings, taglines, and flowing prose
  • Use Inter for labels, navigation, captions, and button text
  • Pair rose with warm cream backgrounds — never with bright white
  • Use the logo on cream or dark indigo backgrounds only
  • Maintain generous white space — let elements breathe
  • Use italic weight to signal the inner / reflective tone
  • Write in sentence case for headings; ALL CAPS only for micro-labels

Don't

  • Do not place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds
  • Do not use bold weights for body text — lightness is intentional
  • Do not distort or rotate the logo
  • Do not use rose and indigo at equal saturation side by side
  • Do not use more than two typeface families
  • Do not write in triads or negations — "not X, but Y" breaks the voice
  • Do not mix day-palette and night-palette colors in the same section

Voice & Tone

Qualities

  • Warm without being soft
  • Contemplative without being abstract
  • Confident without claiming
  • Inviting, never instructing

Writing rules

  • One sentence — one idea
  • Short. Concrete. No hedging.
  • Define by what something is, never by what it is not
  • Avoid triads of parallel phrases
  • End on arrival, never on a door left ajar
